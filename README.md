# Menubar

A configurable menu bar for **World of Warcraft: Forever**, inspired by Macintosh desktops and Warcraft's own interface.

Put a bar at the top, bottom, or both edges of the screen. Arrange widgets on the left, centre or right, and choose a Mac OS 7.6 (Harmony), Mac OS 8.1 (Platinum), Mac OS X (Aqua), macOS 27 (Liquid Glass) or Warcraft appearance. Adjust the height, fonts and colours to suit your interface. Each theme in an appearance profile remembers its own bar and popup fonts, text colours and background opacity. Use **Menus and tooltips** to adjust the widget popups.

The bundle includes thirteen optional widgets: **Ammo, Currency, Durability, Eyes, Location, Mail, Network, Performance, Reputation, Social, Time, Volume and XP**. Enable the widget addons you want, then configure their displays in Menubar's settings. Layouts and widget preferences are saved per character; appearance profiles are shared across your characters.

## Getting started

1. Extract the provided Menubar ZIP into your Forever client's `Interface/AddOns` folder. The `Menubar` and `Menubar_*` folders should sit directly inside it.
2. Restart the client and enable **Menubar** and your chosen widgets.
3. Type `/menubar` or `/mbar`, or select **Menubar** under **Options → AddOns**.

Use the widget list to add, remove, arrange and configure your displays. When updating, replace the addon folders; your saved settings are retained.

## Controls

- Hover over a widget for information.
- Right-click a widget to open its settings.
- Shift-left-click a widget for its contextual menu.

## Reporting issues

Please report issues with your addon version, other installed addons and their versions, client build, affected widget and settings, steps to reproduce and any error text.

For Currency issues involving bags or the bank, also include whether combined bags were enabled.
