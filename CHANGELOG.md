# Changelog

## 0.12.14 — Preview

- Location keeps the zone name and neighbouring widgets in place as coordinates change, including with proportional fonts.

## 0.12.13 — Preview

- Warcraft Brown now starts with 90% bar opacity and 14-point text in widget menus and tooltips.

## 0.12.12 — Preview

- Each theme remembers its bar and popup typography and opacity within an appearance profile.
- Panel font size now supports the mouse wheel, arrow buttons and typed values. Step below 8 or clear the field to return to the theme default.

## 0.12.11 — Preview

- Location shows the instance type when territory is unavailable inside a dungeon or raid. Coordinates appear when the client reports a position on the instance map.

## 0.12.10 — Preview

- Adds independent font, size and text colour settings for widget menus and tooltips. Each starts with the theme’s original appearance.
- The Volume panel adjusts its layout to fit the chosen font.

## 0.12.9 — Preview

- Adds separate opacity sliders for the bar and for widget menus and tooltips. Existing profiles keep their previous popup opacity.

## 0.12.8 — Preview

- Removes the embossed text effect from Mac widget menus and tooltips. Liquid Glass uses near-black labels, dark hints and grey disabled choices.

## 0.12.7 — Preview

- Adds a contrasting text shadow to translucent widget menus and tooltips to keep lettering legible over the game interface.
- Adds **Use full opacity for widget popups** to give menus and tooltips their own full-strength background while keeping the bar translucent.

## 0.12.6 — Preview

- Gives Liquid Glass widget tooltips and menus the same rounded corners and brighter white fill at the selected background opacity.
- Removes the outlines from the Network and Performance icons on Mac OS 8.1 (Platinum).

## 0.12.5 — Preview

- Refines all four Mac widget menu styles, from Harmony's black selection and Platinum's silver face to Aqua pinstripes and rounded Liquid Glass menus.
- Applies the profile’s background opacity to widget dropdowns, context menus and tooltips. Borders, text, checkmarks and highlighted choices keep their contrast.
- Gives the Platinum bar a flatter silver finish and keeps classic Mac menu buttons highlighted while open.
- Network uses a Wi-Fi symbol on Aqua and Liquid Glass and an original TCP/IP-inspired icon on Harmony.
- Refines Durability warning text on light Mac bars and gives Aqua and Liquid Glass a monochrome repair hammer.
- Gives Aqua a hollow Social bell and removes the white outline around its Performance icon.

## 0.12.4 — Preview

- Durability uses a repair hammer and outlined text. Warnings show each affected slot by name and percentage.

## 0.12.3 — Preview

- Removes the decorative tiles behind the Volume, Social and Mail icons.

## 0.12.2 — Preview

- Sets Liquid Glass to 45% background opacity with no border.
- Makes Friendly territory easier to read on dark bars.

## 0.12.1 — Preview

- Gives Harmony and Platinum coloured pixel artwork and bevelled Control Strip tiles for Volume, Mail and Social.
- Refines the classic Mac menus and the Mac OS 8.1 Platinum bar.

## 0.12.0 — Preview

- Adds the macOS 27 (Liquid Glass) theme.
- Gives Volume, Mail and Social original icons for each Mac theme.
- Styles menus and tooltips opened from Menubar to match the active theme.

## 0.11.31 — Preview

## 0.11.30 — Preview

- Corrects asset and territory-data provenance references.

## 0.11.27 — Preview

- Adds a Mac OS X (Aqua) bar style with soft shading and subtle pinstripes.

## 0.11.26 — Preview

- Matches the project icon’s gaze to the Eyes widget.

## 0.11.25 — Preview

## 0.11.24 — Preview

## 0.11.21 — Preview

Menubar preview for World of Warcraft: Forever.

- Customizable top and bottom bars with classic Macintosh and Warcraft appearances.
- Thirteen optional widgets, including XP and reputation gauges, game-volume controls and cursor-following eyes.
- WoW-style menus, tooltips and settings, with per-character widget layouts and shared appearance profiles.
